Christmas Cleaning on Cape Cod: Getting the House Ready for December Guests

Adriana, Super Cleaning CC· September 23, 2026·6 min read

Christmas on Cape Cod has its own rhythm. Seasonal families come back to homes that have been closed since October. Year-round residents host relatives for several days, not just a meal. And the house is full of things it is not full of the rest of the year: a tree, decorations, extra furniture, wrapping paper, and guests. Cleaning for Christmas works best when it happens in a specific order, and the order starts before the first decoration goes up.

Christmas 2026

Christmas Day is Friday, December 25, 2026. The most requested pre-Christmas cleaning dates on Cape Cod are December 18–23. Book by Thanksgiving to secure them.

Step One: Clean Before You Decorate

This is the single most useful piece of advice for a December clean. Once the tree is up and the mantel is dressed, a thorough clean becomes nearly impossible. Floors under the tree, the corner where it stands, the mantel surface, window sills where lights will go, the bookshelves where garland will sit: clean all of it first. The best window is the first or second weekend of December, before decorating begins.

Step Two: If the House Was Closed, Open It Properly

For seasonal Cape Cod homeowners returning for the holidays, a home that has been closed since October needs a real opening clean, not a touch-up. Expect dust on every surface, a musty smell in closed rooms, dead insects on sills, and possibly mildew in bathrooms if the heat was set low. A proper opening covers:

  • Every surface dusted, including light fixtures, fans and the tops of door frames
  • Bathrooms scrubbed with attention to grout and drains that have been dry for weeks
  • Refrigerator cleaned and restarted; kitchen cabinets wiped before restocking
  • All bedding laundered; mattresses aired
  • Windows opened to air the house while heat comes up
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped throughout

Schedule this for the day before you arrive, or the day you arrive, so the house is ready when the family walks in. Our seasonal cleaning page covers what a full opening includes.

Step Three: The Pre-Guest Clean

For a year-round Cape Cod home, or a seasonal home already opened, the pre-guest clean in the week before Christmas focuses on the same rooms that matter at Thanksgiving: guest bedrooms, guest bathrooms, the kitchen, and the living and dining rooms. The difference in December is duration. Guests often stay three to five days, so the guest bathroom and bedroom need to hold up to real use, not a single night.

  • Guest bathroom deep cleaned and stocked for several days
  • Guest bedroom with fresh linens, cleared closet, extra blankets
  • Kitchen with a clean oven and an emptied refrigerator ready for holiday cooking
  • Living room upholstery vacuumed; floors cleaned around, not under, the decorated areas
  • Entryway cleared for coats, boots and luggage

Step Four: Plan the Post-Christmas Reset Now

A house that hosts for a week over Christmas needs a full reset afterward, and the days between Christmas and New Year's are when most Cape Cod families want it done. Book the post-holiday clean at the same time as the pre-Christmas one. Frequently Asked Questions

Should I clean before or after putting up Christmas decorations?

Before. Once the tree and decorations are up, floors, mantels, sills and shelves become hard to reach. Do the thorough clean in early December, then decorate, then do only a light touch-up before guests arrive.

How do I open a Cape Cod house that has been closed since fall for Christmas?

Schedule a full opening clean for the day before or the day you arrive. It should cover every surface, all bathrooms, the kitchen, all bedding and the floors, with windows opened to air the house while the heat comes up.

When should I book Christmas cleaning on Cape Cod?

By Thanksgiving. December 18 to 23 are the most requested dates of the year and fill well in advance. Contact Adriana for a personalized, no-obligation quote.

Do you clean between Christmas and New Year's?

Yes. Post-Christmas resets between December 26 and January 5 are available and are often easier to schedule than pre-Christmas dates.
