End of Season Cleaning for Cape Cod Vacation Rentals: How to Close Your Airbnb for Winter

Adriana, Super Cleaning CC· September 23, 2026·7 min read

Most Cape Cod vacation rentals take their last guests between Columbus Day and early November. What happens in the weeks after that checkout determines two things: whether the property comes through winter without damage, and how fast it can be ready for the first spring booking. An end-of-season clean for a rental is not the same as a fall closing for a private home. Rentals see far more traffic, contain more textiles and consumables, and need to reopen on a schedule rather than when the owner feels like it.

Host timing

Block your calendar for at least two days after the final checkout. One for the end-of-season deep clean, one for winterization and a walk-through. Trying to squeeze both into a single turnover window is where things get missed.

Why Rentals Need a Different Closing Process

A family vacation home might host a few dozen people over a summer. A well-booked Cape Cod Airbnb hosts hundreds. Every set of guests brings sand, salt, sunscreen, cooking, and a slightly different way of using the house. By October, the property carries a season of accumulated wear that regular turnovers were never designed to address. If that wear sits in a closed-up house for five months, it becomes stains that set, odors that linger and mildew that spreads.

The End-of-Season Rental Cleaning Checklist

Textiles and soft goods

  • All linens, towels, mattress protectors and pillow protectors laundered and stored in sealed bins, not left on beds
  • Comforters, blankets and throw pillows washed or dry cleaned; anything stained beyond recovery replaced now, not in May
  • Curtains and slipcovers laundered
  • Mattresses vacuumed, spot-treated and left uncovered to breathe

Kitchen

  • All food and opened consumables removed; sealed pantry staples checked for expiration
  • Refrigerator and freezer emptied, cleaned, unplugged and propped open
  • Oven, microwave, toaster, coffee maker and dishwasher deep cleaned
  • Every drawer and cabinet emptied of crumbs and wiped
  • Inventory of dishes, glassware and cookware taken so replacements can be ordered over winter

Bathrooms

  • Grout and caulk scrubbed; any failing caulk noted for repair
  • Drains cleared, exhaust fans cleaned
  • Guest toiletries and consumables removed
  • Shower curtains and liners laundered or replaced

Whole house

  • Sand removed from door tracks, closets, under rugs and furniture
  • Walls spot-cleaned for scuffs and handprints; touch-up paint list created
  • Outdoor furniture cleaned and stored; grill cleaned and covered; outdoor shower winterized
  • Condition report documenting damage, worn items and maintenance needs

Winterization Comes After the Clean, Not Before

Hosts sometimes have a plumber winterize the property first and then try to clean around drained fixtures. Do it the other way around. A full clean needs running water and a working dishwasher and washing machine. Schedule the end-of-season clean, then the winterization, then a final walk-through with heat set to a safe minimum, water shut off or pipes drained, and every window and door confirmed locked.

Use the Off-Season to Fix What Summer Broke

The condition report from your end-of-season clean is the most useful document you will get all year. It tells you which mattresses need replacing, which caulk is failing, which furniture is worn, which appliances are struggling. Winter is when you have time to address those items without blocking bookings. Hosts who handle repairs in January reopen in May with a property that earns better reviews than one patched together the week before the first guest.

Staying Open Through Winter?

A growing number of Cape Cod rentals stay open year-round for off-season travelers, remote workers and holiday visitors. If that is your plan, the end-of-season deep clean still applies, but instead of closing afterward, you move to a lighter recurring cleaning schedule between the less frequent winter bookings. Holiday weeks in particular fill fast, and a rental that is spotless for Thanksgiving and Christmas guests stands out in a quiet market.

Frequently Asked Questions

When should I close my Cape Cod Airbnb for the season?

Most hosts close between mid-October and early November, after the last fall bookings. Book your end-of-season clean for the days immediately after final checkout so the property is not left sitting dirty.

What is the difference between a turnover clean and an end-of-season clean?

A turnover clean resets the property between guests and focuses on surfaces, linens and restocking. An end-of-season clean goes inside appliances, under furniture, into grout and tracks, and launders every textile in the house. It also produces a condition report for the off-season.

Should I leave the heat on in a closed Cape Cod rental?

Yes, at a safe minimum, typically 50 to 55°F, unless the property is fully drained and winterized by a plumber. Ask your plumber or property manager which approach fits your plumbing and insurance requirements.
