Cleaning Services in Barnstable and Yarmouth, MA: A Local Guide

Adriana, Super Cleaning CC· August 5, 2026·5 min read

Barnstable and Yarmouth are two of the most active cleaning service markets on Cape Cod. Together they form the heart of mid-Cape, with thousands of vacation rentals, seasonal homes and year-round residences spread across communities from Hyannis to Yarmouth Port. Here is everything local property owners need to know about professional cleaning in Barnstable and Yarmouth.

Quick Answer

Super Cleaning CC serves all of Barnstable (including Hyannis, Osterville, Centerville, Cotuit) and all of Yarmouth (Yarmouth Port, South Yarmouth, West Yarmouth, Bass River). Airbnb turnovers, vacation home cleaning and residential cleaning available. Free quote.

Barnstable: Cape Cod's Largest Town

The town of Barnstable is the largest municipality on Cape Cod and encompasses seven distinct villages, each with its own character and cleaning service profile:

  • Hyannis: The commercial hub of the Cape, with high-turnover vacation rentals near the ferry terminals and Main Street, plus commercial cleaning needs for offices and businesses.
  • Osterville and Cotuit: Upscale enclaves with larger estate-style properties that command detailed, thorough cleaning to the standards their owners expect.
  • Centerville: A mix of family vacation homes and year-round residents, active in the Airbnb market during summer.
  • Marstons Mills and West Barnstable: More residential in character, with a growing year-round population and steady demand for regular home cleaning.

Yarmouth: The Mid-Cape Rental Hub

Yarmouth is one of the most concentrated short-term rental markets on Cape Cod. The Route 28 corridor through South and West Yarmouth is lined with rental cottages, motels and vacation properties that generate consistent turnover cleaning demand throughout the summer season.

  • Yarmouth Port: Historic village character with antique shops and B&Bs. Cleaning needs tend toward detailed, careful work in older properties.
  • South Yarmouth: High density of vacation rentals near the beach areas. Saturday turnover demand is intense during July and August.
  • West Yarmouth and Bass River: Mix of vacation cottages and residential neighborhoods with both Airbnb and residential cleaning needs.
