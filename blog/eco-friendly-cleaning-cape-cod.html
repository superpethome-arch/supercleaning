Eco-Friendly Cleaning on Cape Cod: Why It Matters

Adriana — Super Cleaning CC · January 30, 2025 · 5 min read

Cape Cod is home to one of the most ecologically sensitive coastal environments in the United States. Cape Cod Bay, the National Seashore, and the region's ponds and estuaries depend on the health of the surrounding watershed. What we use to clean our homes and vacation properties has a direct impact on this environment. Here is why eco-friendly cleaning matters on Cape Cod — and what it actually means in practice.

The Problem with Conventional Cleaning Products

Standard commercial cleaning products often contain ingredients that are harmful when they enter waterways:

  • Phosphates: Found in many dishwasher and laundry detergents, phosphates accelerate algae growth in ponds and bays — a major problem for Cape Cod's fragile water bodies.
  • Triclosan and antibacterial agents: These compounds can disrupt aquatic ecosystems and contribute to antibiotic resistance.
  • Synthetic fragrances and VOCs: Volatile organic compounds off-gas into indoor air and eventually the outdoor environment.
  • Bleach: When bleach enters the septic system (common on Cape Cod where most properties are not on municipal sewer), it can disrupt beneficial bacteria and eventually reach groundwater.

What Eco-Friendly Cleaning Looks Like

Eco-friendly cleaning does not mean less effective cleaning. Modern plant-based cleaning products clean as effectively as conventional alternatives without the environmental cost. In practice, eco-friendly cleaning involves:

  • Plant-based all-purpose cleaners and degreasers
  • Phosphate-free dishwasher and laundry products
  • Fragrance-free options for clients with sensitivities
  • Microfiber cloths that clean effectively with less product
  • Avoiding unnecessary aerosol sprays

Why It Matters Especially for Cape Cod Properties

Most Cape Cod properties rely on private septic systems rather than municipal sewage treatment. Everything that goes down the drain eventually enters the groundwater — and ultimately the bays, ponds and marshes that define the Cape's landscape and ecology.

Massachusetts has increasingly strict regulations around nitrogen loading in Cape Cod waterways, due to the link between septic leachate, nitrogen and harmful algae blooms. Choosing cleaning products that are phosphate-free and biodegradable is one small but meaningful way homeowners and rental property owners can reduce their environmental footprint.

For Vacation Rental Hosts

Many Airbnb guests actively choose properties that list eco-friendly practices. Mentioning in your listing that you use eco-friendly cleaning products is a simple, honest differentiator that appeals to environmentally conscious travelers — a fast-growing segment of the Cape Cod visitor market.
