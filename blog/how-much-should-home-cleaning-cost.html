How Much Should Vacation Home Cleaning Cost on Cape Cod?

November 23, 2026

How much should home cleaning cost? There is no single price, because the cost of cleaning a vacation home on Cape Cod depends on the property, not on a flat rate. A small cottage and a large rental near the water need very different amounts of work. Knowing what shapes the price helps you compare quotes and avoid surprises.

What goes into the cost of cleaning a vacation home?

The main drivers of price are the size of the home, the number of bathrooms, and how much work each visit needs. The usual factors are:

  • Square footage, bedrooms, and bathrooms. More rooms mean more time and supplies.
  • Type of clean. A light refresh costs less than a deep clean or a full turnover.
  • Frequency. Weekly or seasonal service is often priced differently than a one-time visit.
  • Add-ons. Laundry, linens, inside the fridge or oven, and interior windows all add to the total.
  • Condition. A home that has sat closed for the season takes longer than a well-kept one.

No two homes are the same, so a fair price always reflects your home rather than a one-size number pulled from a chart.

Does the type of clean change the price?

Yes, and it is one of the biggest factors. A standard refresh of an already tidy home is the lightest option. A deep clean takes on baseboards, inside appliances, grout, and buildup, so it runs higher. A move-out or turnover clean sits in the same range or above, since the whole home is cleaned as if no one lives there. When you ask for a quote, naming the type of clean you want is the single most useful detail you can give.

Why do vacation rentals price differently than regular homes?

Vacation rentals and Airbnb homes turn over often, sometimes between a same-day check-out and check-in. That tight window, plus fresh linens, restocking, and a careful look for anything guests left behind, adds to the work. One-time or recurring service

How often you book changes the math. A one-time clean is priced on its own, while weekly, biweekly, or seasonal visits are often set up on a regular rate because the home stays easier to maintain between trips. For a rental, many owners schedule a clean after every guest, so the cost follows the booking calendar rather than a fixed monthly figure. If you tell a cleaner your pattern up front, the quote will match how you actually use the home.

What a fair cleaning quote should include

A clear quote lists which rooms and tasks are covered, whether supplies are included, how long the visit should take, and the price for one-time versus recurring service. If one number sits far below the rest, ask what was left out. A clear scope protects both you and the cleaner, and it keeps the final bill from drifting away from what you were quoted.
