Cleaning Cape Cod Vacation Rentals After Large Groups

Adriana, Super Cleaning CC· August 5, 2026·5 min read

Large-group vacation rentals on Cape Cod — properties that sleep 8, 10, 12 or more guests — present cleaning challenges that standard turnover service is not always equipped to handle. From the volume of linens and towels to the state of the kitchen after a week-long family reunion, large-group turnovers require a different approach.

Quick Answer

Large-group Cape Cod rentals (8+ guests) require more time, a larger cleaning team, and more thorough kitchen and bathroom cleaning than standard turnovers. Budget for 6–8 hours for a 5-bedroom large-group property. Communicate the group size to your cleaning service in advance.

What Makes Large-Group Turnovers Different

A week of 12 people in a Cape Cod vacation home generates more wear than a typical family of 4. The differences are significant:

  • Kitchen: After a week of cooking for a large group, the oven, stovetop, grill, and all appliances require thorough cleaning. Grease buildup, food residue, and condiment spills are extensive compared to a smaller-party stay.
  • Bathrooms: Multiple bathrooms serving many guests accumulate soap scum, hair, mildew and residue at a much higher rate. Every bathroom needs full attention, not a quick wipe-down.
  • Linens: 6-8 beds times 2-3 sets of linens each is a significant laundry and logistics challenge. If you use a linen service, ensure they know the full group size and bed count.
  • Outdoor areas: Large groups use outdoor spaces intensively — decks, patios, grills, fire pits, outdoor showers. These areas require significantly more time to reset than a standard turnover.
  • Sand: More feet in and out of the beach means exponentially more sand on every floor surface, entryway and bathroom.

What to Tell Your Cleaning Service

Before a large-group turnover, communicate:

  • The exact number of guests in the departing group
  • Whether the property was used for a specific event (reunion, birthday, wedding guests)
  • Any areas of the property that saw heavy use
  • Your linen situation — service delivery or on-site laundry
  • Whether the next guests are also a large group (affects restocking)

Frequently Asked Questions: Large-Group Cleaning on Cape Cod

How long does it take to clean a large Cape Cod vacation rental?

A 4-5 bedroom property that has hosted 10-12 guests for a week typically requires 6–8 hours for a professional team. A 6-7 bedroom property may require more. Budget accordingly when setting check-in and checkout times.

Should I charge a higher cleaning fee for large groups?

Yes. If your property frequently hosts large groups, your Airbnb or VRBO cleaning fee should reflect the additional time and labor required. A cleaning fee of $350–$500 for a large Cape Cod property is reasonable and expected by guests booking at those sizes.

Do you bring extra staff for large-group turnovers?

Yes. Super Cleaning CC coordinates team size based on property size and group count. For large-group turnovers, we ensure enough staff are present to meet the turnover window without sacrificing cleaning quality.
