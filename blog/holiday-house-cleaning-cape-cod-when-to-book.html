Holiday House Cleaning in Cape Cod: When Should You Book?

Adriana, Super Cleaning CC· September 23, 2026·5 min read

Every year the same thing happens. A Cape Cod homeowner decides in mid-November that they would like the house professionally cleaned before Thanksgiving, calls around, and finds that every reputable service is booked through the holiday. The same pattern repeats the week before Christmas. Holiday cleaning on Cape Cod is a capacity problem: a small number of trusted teams, a large number of homes hosting on the same three or four days. Here is how the calendar actually works and when to book.

Holiday dates 2026

Thanksgiving: Thursday, November 26. Christmas: Friday, December 25. New Year's Day: Friday, January 1, 2027. The busiest cleaning weeks are November 16–25 and December 14–24.

Thanksgiving: Book by Late October

Pre-Thanksgiving cleans concentrate in the ten days before the holiday, roughly November 16 through 25. Most Cape Cod homeowners want the clean done between the weekend before and the Wednesday of Thanksgiving week. Those dates go first. To secure one, book by the last week of October. Post-Thanksgiving resets on Friday, Saturday or the following Monday are easier to schedule but still fill.

Christmas: Book by Thanksgiving

December is the tightest month of the year. Pre-Christmas cleans cluster between December 14 and 24, and many families want the same two or three days: the weekend before Christmas and December 23. A useful rule: if you have not booked your Christmas clean by Thanksgiving, you are relying on cancellations. Booking both holidays at the same time in October is the simplest way to guarantee your dates.

New Year's: The Easiest to Book, the Most Overlooked

Post-holiday resets between December 26 and January 5 have more availability than the pre-holiday weeks, and they are often the most satisfying clean of the season: guests gone, decorations coming down, a full month of hosting cleaned out in one visit. Many Cape Cod homeowners use it as the starting point for a recurring cleaning schedule in the new year.

Why Cape Cod Books Up Faster Than Elsewhere

Three reasons. First, the pool of experienced, insured cleaning teams on the Cape is small relative to the number of homes. Second, many homes here host overnight guests, so the job is bigger and takes longer. Third, seasonal homeowners often return for the holidays and need a full opening clean, not just a touch-up, which uses more of the available capacity. The result is that the same ten days in November and December are oversubscribed every year.

What to Have Ready When You Book

  • The date guests arrive and the date they leave
  • Number of bedrooms and bathrooms that will be used
  • Whether the house has been closed since summer or lived in year-round
  • Any specific concerns: oven, guest bathroom, a room that needs airing out
  • Whether you want a post-holiday reset as well

Frequently Asked Questions

When should I book Thanksgiving cleaning on Cape Cod?

By the last week of October. The days between November 16 and 25 are the most requested of the fall and fill first.

When should I book Christmas cleaning on Cape Cod?

By Thanksgiving at the latest. December 14 through 24 is the busiest cleaning stretch of the year. Booking Thanksgiving and Christmas together in October is the most reliable approach.

Is it too late to book holiday cleaning in December?

Not always, but availability depends on cancellations. Contact Adriana directly; post-holiday dates between December 26 and January 5 are usually easier to secure than pre-Christmas dates.

How much does holiday cleaning cost on Cape Cod?
