Spring Opening Checklist for Your Cape Cod Seasonal Home

Adriana, Super Cleaning CC · March 18, 2025 · 7 min read

For the estimated 35,000 seasonal homes on Cape Cod, spring opening is one of the most important events of the year. A property that has been closed since October needs more than a quick sweep. Here is a comprehensive spring opening checklist for Cape Cod seasonal homeowners.

When to Open Your Cape Cod Seasonal Home

Most Cape Cod seasonal properties open between late April and Memorial Day weekend (the last Monday in May). If you rent the property, many owners aim to be ready by May 15 to capture late-May bookings. If it is for personal use, Memorial Day is the traditional target.

Book your spring opening cleaning service as early as February or March. The best cleaning services fill up quickly for the pre-season rush.

Before You Arrive: What to Do Remotely

  • Contact your plumber to confirm pipes were properly winterized
  • Check with your property manager or neighbor on any winter storm damage
  • Schedule your cleaning service, pest control and any maintenance appointments before you open
  • Order any supplies you want stocked on arrival

The Full Spring Opening Cleaning Checklist

Every Room

  • Remove dust covers from furniture
  • Vacuum and wipe all surfaces, dust accumulates heavily in closed spaces
  • Clean all light fixtures and ceiling fans
  • Wipe down baseboards and door frames
  • Clean all windows inside
  • Check for signs of pests or rodent activity

Kitchen

  • Run the dishwasher empty with a cleaning tablet
  • Clean the inside of the refrigerator, remove old items and wipe down shelves
  • Clean the oven inside and out
  • Wipe down all cabinets inside and out
  • Check under the sink for any moisture or leaks
  • Run water through all faucets and the garbage disposal

Bathrooms

  • Run all faucets and flush toilets to clear pipes
  • Check for any mineral deposits or staining from winter
  • Deep scrub the toilet, shower and tub
  • Check grout for any mold growth from winter humidity
  • Restock toiletries and fresh towels

Outdoor Areas

  • Sweep deck and porch thoroughly
  • Wipe down outdoor furniture
  • Check outdoor lighting fixtures
  • Clear any debris from gutters and downspouts
  • Check the outdoor shower for any winter damage

What Professionals Handle Differently

A professional spring opening clean goes deeper than a homeowner clean. An experienced Cape Cod cleaning service will know to check behind appliances, inside vents, and in areas where mold or pests might have settled over winter. They will also be able to document any issues they find, valuable for remote owners who cannot inspect the property themselves.

After the Cleaning: Final Checks

  • Run all appliances through a cycle to confirm they are working
  • Test smoke and carbon monoxide detectors
  • Check the water heater is functioning
  • Confirm internet and cable are working if guests depend on them
  • Photograph the property condition for your records

A well-executed spring opening sets the tone for the entire season. Properties that open clean, fresh and well-maintained earn better reviews, more bookings, and longer-term rental relationships.
