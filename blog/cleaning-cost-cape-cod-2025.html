What Affects the Price of Cleaning on Cape Cod

The price you pay for a professional clean on Cape Cod depends on several factors that go beyond square footage:

  • Property condition: A property that has not been professionally cleaned in several months costs more to bring to standard than one on a regular cleaning schedule. Initial cleans or deep cleans always cost more than maintenance cleans.
  • Frequency: Recurring weekly or bi-weekly clients typically pay 10–20% less per visit than one-time bookings. A cleaning service that knows your property and keeps it maintained can work more efficiently.
  • Seasonal demand: July and August are peak season on Cape Cod. Some cleaning services charge a premium during peak weeks, particularly for weekend turnovers around July 4th and Labor Day.
  • Extras: Inside oven cleaning, inside refrigerator, window cleaning, exterior pressure washing and laundry services are typically priced separately.
  • Location on the Cape: Properties in Falmouth, Barnstable and Sandwich are typically within 15–20 minutes of most cleaning teams. Properties in Wellfleet, Truro or Provincetown on the Outer Cape may carry a travel surcharge.

Standard Clean vs. Deep Clean: Which Do You Need?

A standard clean maintains a property that is already in good condition. It covers surfaces, bathrooms, kitchen, floors and bedrooms to a clean and presentable standard. A standard clean is appropriate for a home that is cleaned regularly.

A deep clean addresses areas that standard cleaning does not reach: inside ovens, refrigerators, behind appliances, grout lines, baseboards, ceiling fans, window sills and inside cabinets. A deep clean is appropriate for a property that has not been cleaned professionally in some time, a seasonal property being opened for summer, or a home being prepared for sale or new tenants.

For most Cape Cod vacation rentals, the first clean of the season should be a deep clean, followed by standard turnovers throughout the summer.
