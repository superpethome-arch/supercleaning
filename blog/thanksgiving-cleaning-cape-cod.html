Holiday Cleaning

How to Get Your Cape Cod Home Ready for Thanksgiving Guests

Adriana, Super Cleaning CC· September 23, 2026·6 min read

Thanksgiving is the one weekend a year when the whole family sees every room of the house. Guest bedrooms that have been closed since summer, the bathroom nobody uses, the oven that has not been deep cleaned since the Fourth of July. On Cape Cod, where many homes double as the family gathering place for relatives coming from Boston, Providence and beyond, getting the house ready is a real project. Here is how to plan it so the week of Thanksgiving is about cooking and family, not scrubbing.

Thanksgiving 2026

Thanksgiving falls on Thursday, November 26, 2026. Professional cleaners across Cape Cod book up for the week of November 16 to 25 well in advance. If you want help, reserve your date in October.

Two Weeks Out: The Deep Work

This is when the heavy, disruptive cleaning happens, early enough that the house can recover and the results still hold on the day.

  • Oven and stovetop: a full interior degrease now, so the turkey does not set off the smoke alarm and the kitchen does not smell like August's cookouts.
  • Refrigerator: emptied, shelves washed, expired items gone. You will need every inch of space the week of.
  • Guest bedrooms: mattresses vacuumed, linens laundered, closets cleared of storage, windows opened to air out rooms that have been closed since summer.
  • Guest bathroom: grout, caulk, exhaust fan, behind the toilet. The bathroom guests use is the one they judge.
  • Dining room: chandelier or light fixture dusted, chairs wiped, table leaves located and cleaned, china and serving pieces washed.

One Week Out: The Whole-House Reset

  • Floors throughout: vacuumed and mopped, rugs cleaned
  • Baseboards, window sills, door frames and light switches wiped
  • Living room upholstery vacuumed, cushions fluffed, throws laundered
  • Entryway cleared and mudroom organized for a pile of coats and boots
  • Windows cleaned inside, especially in the dining and living rooms where daylight falls on the table

The Day Before: Finishing Touches

  • Bathrooms wiped down and restocked: fresh hand towels, soap, extra toilet paper visible
  • Kitchen counters cleared and sanitized, sink empty, dishwasher empty
  • Quick vacuum of high-traffic paths and the dining room
  • Guest room beds made, a glass and water on the nightstand, closet space cleared
  • Trash and recycling emptied with fresh bags, extra bags where guests can find them

What to Leave to a Professional

Most families can handle the day-before touches. The two-week-out deep work is where professional help earns its keep: the oven, the refrigerator, the guest bathroom grout, the mattresses. A single pre-holiday clean in mid-November covers all of it in one visit and leaves you with a house that only needs light maintenance until the doorbell rings. For Cape Cod homeowners who also host Christmas, booking both visits at once is the simplest approach.

Do Not Forget the Day After

The kitchen after Thanksgiving is a job in itself. Many Cape Cod families book a post-holiday clean for the Friday or Saturday after, so the house resets before the December calendar begins. If relatives are staying through the weekend, a Monday reset works just as well.

Frequently Asked Questions

When should I book a pre-Thanksgiving cleaning on Cape Cod?

Reserve your date in October. The week before Thanksgiving is one of the busiest of the year for Cape Cod cleaning services, and mid-November availability disappears fast.

What should be cleaned before Thanksgiving guests arrive?

Prioritize the kitchen (oven, refrigerator, counters), the guest bathroom, guest bedrooms and the dining and living rooms. Those are the rooms every guest will use and notice.

How far in advance should a deep clean happen before Thanksgiving?

About one to two weeks before. That leaves time for the house to settle, for any repairs the clean reveals, and for a light touch-up the day before.
